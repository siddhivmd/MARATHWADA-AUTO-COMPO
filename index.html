About MACPL

At Marathwada Auto Compo, we manufacture auto components required at large scale.

Our expertise lies in fast development of components and their manufacturing lines as per the customer’s specifications and requirements. We develop complex, technically challenging auto components that contribute immensely to the safety of a vehicle — backed by state-of-the-art equipment and well-controlled processes.
