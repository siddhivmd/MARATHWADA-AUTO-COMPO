Our Overview

Marathwada Auto Compo Pvt. Ltd. is where we develop complex, technically challenging auto components that contribute immensely to the safety of a vehicle.

State-of-the-art equipment and well-controlled processes ensure process and product quality — the most crucial of all factors. MACPL was started in 1997 by two technocrats, Ram Bhogale joined by Ajit Soundalgekar, to manufacture steering columns for scooters and 3-wheel commercial and passenger vehicles.

By 2000, MACPL became renowned for its ability to develop products and manufacturing lines within record time. Today, 14 OEMs enjoy our reliable, hassle-free and proactive component development. Systems like TPM and TPO have contributed to improving our shop-floor efficiency. Appreciated by many auto majors across the country, we continue to grow in many dimensions.

Quality & Systems

Manufacturing Systems

For the last 10 years, MACPL has invested time and resources in adopting various manufacturing management systems. MACPL manages its shop floor and production equipment using Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) techniques, delivering benefits across Product, Quality, Cost, Delivery, Safety and Morale (PQCDSM) parameters.

The AITG Group

Keeping Operational Excellence and Innovation in focus, the first company of the group, Nirlep Appliances, was started in 1968. Since then, the group has diversified into consumer goods, aluminium rolling, specialty paints and coatings, dairy and chemical equipment, and auto components.

The Applied Innovation & Technology Group (AITG) brings these companies together with common values and shared resources in HR, IT and finance — sharing best practices and bringing uniformity to management processes.
