MACPL: Now with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 & OHSAS 18001 Certification

We, AITG – Marathwada Auto Compo Pvt Ltd, are committed towards:

  1. Satisfying customer requirements by achieving perfection, consistency in quality and continual improvement in processes.
  2. Conserving the environment by continually optimising the use of water, energy and other resources, along with preventing water, land and air pollution.
  3. Continually improving occupational health and safety management systems and performance, to provide a safe and healthy workplace to our employees and stakeholders.
  4. Complying with all legal requirements to which the organisation subscribes.

…through the collective application of knowledge, skill and effort from management and employees. We are therefore an ISO 9001, ISO 14001 & OHSAS 18001 certified organisation. Implementation of these standards was done at Marathwada with the help of our auditors Mr. Ajaykumar Shinde and Mr. Atul Joshi from TÜV Nord.

Go Green Initiative

MACPL Team Celebrates World Environment Day

As a “Go Green Initiative” of the group, the MACPL team planted around 130 trees on the occasion of World Environment Day 2016. Staff and team members volunteered wholeheartedly. The trees were planted alongside the main road of MIDC Waluj, with plant selection, spacing and row selection by plant height in line with the guidelines released by MIDC / MECC.

MACPL is committed to its responsibility towards society and the environment, and this initiative is a glimpse of the same. We plan to plant a total of 500 trees in phases, covering a total area of 7,000 sq. metres — 1,500 sq. metres were covered in the first phase. Prayas Foundation helped us in this initiative, and the plants were sourced from East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh.
